For you, Shaanussri

I miss you.

My dearest Shaanussri,

There is something I wish I could explain in the simplest and most honest way: I miss you.

Not just because I miss being loved by you. Not just because I miss having someone to call mine, or the comfort of being in a relationship. I miss you — your presence, your voice, our conversations, your laughter, your little habits, and the ordinary moments that became special simply because they were shared with you.

I miss the person who made an ordinary day feel a little brighter. I miss the small things we may not have realised were becoming memories. Even when I am busy, there are moments when something reminds me of you, and for a second my heart wishes you were right there.

You are not replaceable by attention, affection, or simply being in a relationship. It is you, Shaanussri, whom I miss. The person you are. The connection we shared. The memories that belong to us.

With love, Sathish ♡

The truth, without excuses

Our arguments are not our whole story.

I know our arguments hurt you. I know there were moments when words, misunderstandings and silence left wounds. I will not minimise what you felt, and I will not use our happy memories as an excuse for the times I hurt you.

But beyond those difficult moments, we also had something beautiful. We laughed. We went on dates. We made memories. We shared little moments that nobody else could understand in quite the same way, because they were ours.

I wish we could remember that the difficult chapters do not erase every beautiful page that came before them. I am not asking you to forget the hurt. I only hope you can remember that our story held happiness and genuine care too.

Taking responsibility

I want my actions to speak.

I know my silence hurt you. When you needed communication, I sometimes withdrew instead. I may have thought staying quiet would prevent another argument, but I understand that silence can feel like being ignored, left alone, or made to carry everything by yourself.

I am genuinely sorry for the hurt that caused.

I am taking responsibility for my mistakes. I have started working on the way I communicate, the way I respond when things are difficult, and the way I handle my emotions. I am not saying I have become perfect overnight. I know change is proven through consistent actions, especially when things are not easy.

I am changing not simply to win you back or because I am afraid of losing you. I am changing because I want to become a better person, because our relationship deserves healthier communication, and because I want a future where we both feel heard, respected and emotionally safe.

I know trust and healing may take time. I will not demand that you believe me just because I have written these words. I want my actions, patience and consistency to show you that I mean them.

“I cannot rewrite the moments when I got it wrong. But I can take responsibility for what comes next, and keep choosing to do better.”

A hope, not a demand

If we write another chapter…

I hope it is gentler. More honest. More patient. A chapter where we listen before we react, speak instead of shutting down, and make room for each other's feelings.
